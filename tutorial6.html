Tutorial 6

OBSESSION FLOW TERMINAL - DECISION INDEX OBSESSION FLOW TERMINAL - መወሰኒ መለክዒ

A bilingual guide that teaches followers how to read your GitHub Decision Index: market bias, execution state, funding, speed, institutional flows, and chart volume. ንተኸታተልትኻ ኣብ GitHub ዘሎ Decision Index ብኸመይ ከም ዝንበብ ዝምህር ናይ እንግሊዝኛን ትግርኛን መመርሒ።

1. HTF Bias & Market Regime 1. ናይ ነዊሕ ግዜ ኣንፈትን ናይ ዕዳጋ ኩነታትን

1

HTF Bias: Bearish

HTF Bias tells you who controls the larger timeframe. When it is bearish, sellers control the main structure and long trades need stronger confirmation.

HTF Bias ኣብ ዓቢ ግዜ መን ከም ዝቆጻጸር የርኢ። Bearish እንተኾይኑ፣ ሸየጥቲ ነቲ ዓቢ ኣቃውማ ይቆጻጸርዎ፣ Long ንምእታው ድማ ዝያዳ ጽኑዕ ምርግጋጽ የድሊ።

Market Regime

Regime describes the environment: trend, range, squeeze, expansion, risk-on, or risk-off. The Decision Index should never be read from one number alone.

Regime ማለት ናይ ዕዳጋ ኩነታት እዩ፤ trend, range, squeeze, expansion, risk-on ወይ risk-off። Decision Index ብሓደ ቁጽሪ ጥራይ ክንበብ የብሉን።

Teaching point: Bias is the map. Execution is the trigger. Never confuse direction with entry. ናይ ትምህርቲ ነጥቢ፦ Bias ካርታ እዩ። Execution ድማ መእተዊ trigger እዩ። ኣንፈትን መእተዊን ኣይትሓዋውስ።

2. Execution State 2. ናይ ኣፈጻጽማ ኩነታት

2

Execution State explains whether the terminal allows action now or whether the trader must wait. It converts the data into a trading condition.

Execution State ሕጂ ምእታው ይፍቀድ ድዩ ወይ ንጽበ ይብል ድዩ ይገልጽ። እቲ ዳታ ናብ ናይ trading ኩነታት ይቕይሮ።

Valid Execution

  • Bias, liquidity, volume, and speed support the same direction.
  • Price is near a planned level, not in the middle of noise.
  • Risk is clear before the trade is taken.
  • Bias, liquidity, volume, speed ኩሎም ኣብ ሓደ ኣንፈት እንተተሰማሚዖም።
  • ዋጋ ኣብ ዝተደለየ መደብ እንተቐሪቡ፣ ኣብ noise ማእከል እንተዘይኮይኑ።
  • Risk ቅድሚ trade ምእታው ግልጺ እንተኾይኑ።

No-Trade / Wait

  • Signals conflict with each other.
  • Funding is crowded but price has not confirmed.
  • Volume is weak or fake breakout behavior appears.
  • መለክዒታት ንሓድሕዶም እንተተጋጭዮም።
  • Funding ጽዑቕ እንተኾይኑ ግን ዋጋ እንተዘየረጋጊጹ።
  • Volume ደኺሙ ወይ fake breakout እንተተራእዩ።
Rule: The Decision Index is not a buy/sell button. It is a filter that tells you when conditions are clean enough to act. ሕጊ፦ Decision Index ናይ buy/sell button ኣይኮነን። ኩነታት ንምእታው ክሳብ ክንደይ ጽሩይ ከም ዝኾነ ዝሕብር filter እዩ።

3. Funding Matrices 3. Funding Matrices / ናይ Funding መለክዒታት

3

Funding Matrices show whether derivatives traders are crowded long or crowded short. They help identify pressure, imbalance, and possible squeeze risk.

Funding Matrices ናይ derivatives traders long ድዮም ብዙሓት ወይ short ድዮም ብዙሓት የርእዩ። ጸቕጢ፣ ዘይሚዛን፣ squeeze risk ንምርዳእ ይሕግዙ።

Long-Crowded

Positive funding with weak price action can warn that too many traders are already long. This can create downside liquidation risk.

Positive funding እንተሃልዩ ግን price action ደኺሙ እንተኾይኑ፣ ብዙሓት ቀዲሞም long ኣትዮም ከም ዘለዉ የጠንቅቕ። እዚ downside liquidation risk ከምጽእ ይኽእል።

Short-Crowded

Negative funding with strong price recovery can warn that shorts are trapped. This can create upside squeeze risk.

Negative funding እንተሃልዩ እሞ ዋጋ ብሓይሊ እንተተመሊሱ፣ shorts ተታሒዞም ከም ዘለዉ የርኢ። እዚ upside squeeze risk ከምጽእ ይኽእል።

Teaching point: Funding does not predict alone. It becomes powerful when it agrees with price rejection, volume, and liquidation zones. ናይ ትምህርቲ ነጥቢ፦ Funding በይኑ ትንበያ ኣይገብርን። ምስ price rejection, volume, liquidation zones እንተተሰማሚዑ ግን ሓይሊ ይረክብ።

4. Speed & Velocity 4. ፍጥነትን Velocityን

4

Speed and velocity explain how fast the market is moving and whether the move is accelerating or losing power. This helps avoid late entries.

Speed ን Velocity ዕዳጋ ብኽንደይ ፍጥነት ይንቀሳቐስ ከም ዘሎ፣ እቲ ምንቅስቓስ ይበርትዕ ድዩ ወይ ይደክም ድዩ ይገልጹ። እዚ ድሕሪ ግዜ ምእታው ንምክልኻል ይሕግዝ።

Acceleration

Acceleration means momentum is building. If it matches bias and volume, continuation probability improves.

Acceleration ማለት momentum ይህነጽ ኣሎ ማለት እዩ። ምስ bias ን volume እንተተሰማሚዑ፣ continuation ዕድል ይውስኽ።

Exhaustion

Exhaustion means price moved too far too fast and new volume is not supporting it. This warns against chasing.

Exhaustion ማለት ዋጋ ብፍጥነት ርሒቑ ከይዱ፣ ሓድሽ volume ግን ኣይድግፎን ኣሎ ማለት እዩ። እዚ chasing ንኸይትገብር የጠንቅቕ።

5. Institutional Flows 5. ትካላዊ ዋሕዚ / Institutional Flows

5

Institutional Flows help explain whether larger players may be accumulating, distributing, hedging, or defending levels. This is where ETF flow, liquidity maps, open interest, and macro data matter.

Institutional Flows ዓበይቲ ተጻወትቲ accumulating, distributing, hedging ወይ ደረጃታት ይከላኸሉ ኣለዉ ድዮም ንምርዳእ ይሕግዝ። ኣብዚ ETF flow, liquidity maps, open interest, macro data ኣገዳሲ ይኸውን።

Accumulation Signs

  • Dips are bought quickly.
  • Volume appears near support.
  • Price holds while bad news fails to push lower.
  • Dips ብፍጥነት እንተተዓዲጎም።
  • Volume ኣብ support እንተተራእዩ።
  • ዋጋ እንተተታሒዙ፣ ሕማቕ ዜና እውን ንታሕቲ ከይደፍኦ።

Distribution Signs

  • Rallies are sold quickly.
  • Volume increases on red candles.
  • Price fails at resistance again and again.
  • Rallies ብፍጥነት እንተተሸይጦም።
  • Volume ኣብ red candles እንተወሲኹ።
  • ዋጋ ኣብ resistance ብተደጋጋሚ እንተፈሺሉ።

6. Chart Volume 6. ናይ Chart Volume ትርጉም

6

Chart Volume confirms whether a move has real participation. A breakout without volume is weaker than a breakout with strong volume confirmation.

Chart Volume ሓደ ምንቅስቓስ ብሓቂ ተሳትፎ ኣለዎ ድዩ የረጋግጽ። Volume ዘይብሉ breakout ካብ strong volume ዘለዎ breakout ዝደኸመ እዩ።

Healthy Volume

Healthy volume supports the direction of price. Green expansion with rising volume can support upside continuation; red expansion with rising volume can support downside continuation.

ጥዑይ volume ንኣንፈት ዋጋ ይድግፍ። Green expansion ምስ rising volume ንላዕሊ continuation ይድግፍ፤ red expansion ምስ rising volume ንታሕቲ continuation ይድግፍ።

Weak / Trap Volume

Weak volume means the move may be a trap. When price breaks a level but volume does not confirm, the Decision Index should warn followers to wait.

ደኺም volume እቲ ምንቅስቓስ trap ክኸውን ከም ዝኽእል የርኢ። ዋጋ level እንተሰቢሩ ግን volume እንተዘየረጋጊጹ፣ Decision Index ንተኸታተልቲ ንጽበ ክብሎም ኣለዎ።

Final lesson: The best reading comes when Bias, Execution State, Funding, Speed, Institutional Flow, and Volume all tell the same story. መወዳእታ ትምህርቲ፦ ዝበለጸ ንባብ ዝመጽእ Bias, Execution State, Funding, Speed, Institutional Flow, Volume ኩሎም ሓደ ታሪኽ እንተነጊሮም እዩ።
