Funding Account Rules Explained Step by Step (ክትፈልጦ ዝግበአካ ቅድሚ ምጅማርካ)

This guide is made for educational purposes. It explains common rules used by many funding systems and prop trading challenges in both English and Tigrigna.

This is not written for one specific company. Always check the official rules of the funding company you use.
ሓደ ነገር አስተውዕል ኢዚኦም ትካላት ትገዝአሎም ተመሳሰልቲ እዮም
Rule 1

Risk Management Conditions / ናይ Risk Management ሕግታት

English

Most funding systems require traders to follow strict risk management rules. These usually include maximum risk per trade, daily drawdown, maximum loss, stop-loss obligation, and responsible trading behavior.

  • Do not risk more than the allowed percentage per position.
  • Use a stop loss on every position.
  • Avoid gambling or all-or-nothing trading behavior.
  • Do not rely on one lucky trading day.
Commissions and fees may be included in risk calculations depending on the funding provider.

ትግርኛ

ብዙሓት Funding Systems ትሬደራት ጽኑዕ ናይ Risk Management ሕግታት ክኽተሉ ይጠልቡ። እዚ ብዙሕ ግዜ Maximum Risk Per Trade፣ Daily Drawdown፣ Maximum Loss፣ Stop-Loss Obligation ከምኡውን Responsible Trading ይሓዝ።

  • ኣብ ሓደ Position ካብ ዝተፈቐደ መጠን ዝበዝሕ Risk ኣይትውሰድ።
  • ኣብ ኩሉ Position Stop Loss ተጠቐም።
  • Gambling ወይ all-or-nothing ኣተሓሳስባ ኣይትጠቐም።
  • ኣብ ሓንቲ ዕድለኛ መዓልቲ ጥራይ ኣይትምርኮስ።
Commission ከምኡውን Fees ኣብ Risk Calculation ክኣትዉ ይኽእሉ፣ እዚ ኣብ ሕጊ ናይቲ Funding Provider ይምርኮስ።
Important Lesson

Trailing Daily Drawdown / ናይ Trailing Daily Drawdown ምርዳእ

English

Many funding systems calculate Daily Drawdown using Equity, not only closed profits and losses.

  • Floating profit counts.
  • Floating loss counts.
  • Daily Drawdown changes throughout the day.
  • If your equity improves, drawdown can decrease.
  • If your equity worsens, drawdown can increase.

Real Example

Situation Daily Drawdown
Position moves against you -15
Position recovers -12
Position falls again -13
Important:
Daily Drawdown is a live value. It can increase or decrease as your account equity changes.

Think of Daily Drawdown as a live risk meter:

  • Better Equity = Smaller Drawdown
  • Worse Equity = Larger Drawdown
If Daily Drawdown reaches the firm's maximum limit, the account may fail.

ትግርኛ

ብዙሓት Funding Systems Daily Drawdown ብ Equity ይሓስቡ፣ ብ Closed Profit ወይ Closed Loss ጥራይ ኣይኮነን።ምሳሌ 2000 ከሲብካ ጽኒሕካ እንድሕር 1000 አጥፊካ ተካሪጅካ አለካ ።

  • Floating Profit ይቑጸር።
  • Floating Loss ይቑጸር።
  • Daily Drawdown ኣብ ውሽጢ ደቃይቅ ክቕየር ይኽእል።
  • Equity እንተ ተመሓየሸ Drawdown ይጎድል።
  • Equity እንተ ወሪዱ Drawdown ይውስኽ።

ናይ ሓቂ ኣብነት

ኩነታት Daily Drawdown
ትሬድ ናብ ክሳራ ይኸይድ -15
ትሬድ ይምለስ -12
ዳግማይ ይወርድ -13
ኣገዳሲ:
Daily Drawdown ህያው (Live) ቁጽሪ እዩ። ብመጠን Equity ኣብ ውሽጢ መዓልቲ ክውስኽ ወይ ክጎድል ይኽእል።

Daily Drawdown ከም ናይ Risk ሚዛን ክትርእዮ ትኽእል፦

  • Equity እንተ ተመሓየሸ → Drawdown ይጎድል
  • Equity እንተ ወሪዱ → Drawdown ይውስኽ
Daily Drawdown ካብ ዝላዓለ መኽሰብ እንተወሪዱ፣ Account ክወድቕ ይኽእል እዩ። ዋላ አብ መክሰብ ከለኻ።
Rule 3

Do I Have to Close Positions Overnight?

English

In many funding systems, overnight and weekend holding may be allowed. This means traders do not always need to close positions before the end of the day.

Always check the specific provider, because some firms restrict overnight or weekend holding.

ትግርኛ

ኣብ ብዙሓት Funding Systems፣ Overnight ከምኡውን Weekend Holding ይፈቅዱ። ማለት ትሬደር ቅድሚ መዓልቲ ምውዳእ Positions ክዓጽዎም ግዴታ የብሉን።

ግን ኩሉ ግዜ ናይቲ Provider ሕጊ ኣረጋግጽ፣ ምኽንያቱ ገለ Firms Overnight ወይ Weekend Holding ይኽልክሉ።
Rule 4

How Many Positions Can I Open?

English

Some funding systems do not limit the number of positions, but the trader must manage total risk. For example, two positions risking 3% each can create 6% daily loss if both fail.

Even if each trade is valid alone, the combined loss may breach daily drawdown.

ትግርኛ

ገለ Funding Systems ብዝሒ Positions ኣይገድቡን፣ ግን ትሬደር ጠቕላላ Risk ክቆጻጸር ኣለዎ። ንኣብነት 2 Positions ነፍሲ ወከፉ 3% Risk እንተ ኾይኑ፣ ክልቲኦም እንተ ኸሲሮም 6% Daily Loss ይፈጥሩ።

ነፍሲ ወከፍ ትሬድ በበይኑ ቅቡል እኳ እንተ ኾነ፣ ጠቕላላ Loss ን Daily Drawdown ክጥሕስ ይኽእል።
Rule 5

Inactivity Rule

English

Many funding accounts have inactivity rules. A common example is that if the most recent trading day is older than 30 days, the account may be disabled.

A trading day usually means the day a trade was closed, not the day it was opened.

ትግርኛ

ብዙሓት Funding Accounts ናይ Inactivity ሕግታት ኣለዎም። ኣብነት፣ ካብ መወዳእታ ዝተዓጽወ ትሬድ 30 መዓልታት እንተ ሓሊፉ፣ ኣካውንት ክዕጾ ወይ ክስንከል ይኽእል።

Trading Day ብዙሕ ግዜ ትሬድ ዝተዓጽወሉ መዓልቲ እዩ፣ ዝተኸፈተሉ መዓልቲ ኣይኮነን።

Rule 6

Funded Account Rules

English

After a trader becomes funded, extra rules may apply. Common funded account limits include maximum margin exposure and maximum total open position value.

  • Maximum exposure may be limited to 25% of initial account balance.
  • Total open position value may be limited to 2× initial account balance.
  • Daily drawdown, maximum loss, risk per trade, and stop-loss rules usually continue.

ትግርኛ

ትሬደር Funded ምስ ኮነ፣ ተወሳኺ ሕግታት ክህልዉ ይኽእሉ። ዝተለመዱ ሕግታት Maximum Margin Exposure ከምኡውን Maximum Total Open Position Value ይሓዙ።

  • Maximum Exposure ክሳብ 25% ናይ መጀመርታ ባላንስ ክግደብ ይኽእል።
  • Total Open Position Value ክሳብ 2× ናይ መጀመርታ ባላንስ ክግደብ ይኽእል።
  • Daily Drawdown፣ Maximum Loss፣ Risk Per Trade፣ Stop-Loss Rules ብዙሕ ግዜ ይቕጽሉ።
Rule 7

If I Break the Rules, Do I Get Another Chance?

English

In many funding systems, if a trader violates a trading objective, that account is invalidated. If the breach happens on a funded account, the agreement may be terminated.

Usually, a trader can start again by buying a new challenge, but repeat attempts may not receive a discount.

ትግርኛ

ኣብ ብዙሓት Funding Systems፣ ትሬደር ናይ Trading Objective ሕጊ እንተ ጥሒሱ፣ እቲ Account ይወድቕ። እቲ ጥሕሰት ኣብ Funded Account እንተ ኾይኑ፣ Agreement ክቋረጽ ይኽእል።

ብዙሕ ግዜ ትሬደር ሓድሽ Challenge ብምግዛእ ዳግማይ ክጅምር ይኽእል፣ ግን Discount ክወሃብ ኣይግድን።
Restriction 1

Prohibited Trading Actions

English

Funding systems may set a maximum capital allocation per user across all active challenges. Some platforms also prohibit actions such as requesting extra demo funds, modifying balances, altering API keys, trading the wrong market type, or combining restricted trading modes.

  • Do not modify account balance.
  • Do not change or delete API keys if prohibited.
  • Do not trade restricted pairs or products.
  • Do not mix spot and margin if the account rules forbid it.

ትግርኛ

Funding Systems ኣብ ኩሎም Active Challenges ንሓደ User ዝፍቀድ Maximum Capital Allocation ክውስኑ ይኽእሉ። ገለ Platforms ተወሳኺ Demo Funds ምሕታት፣ Balance ምቕያር፣ API Key ምቕያር፣ ዘይተፈቐደ Market Type ምትሬድ ወይ Restricted Trading Modes ምሕዋስ ይኽልክሉ።

  • Account Balance ኣይትቐይር።
  • API Keys ምቕያር ወይ ምስራዝ እንተ ተኸልኪሉ ኣይትግበር።
  • Restricted Pairs ወይ Products ኣይትትሬድ።
  • Rules እንተ ከልኪሎም Spot ከምኡውን Margin ኣይትሕወስ።
Restriction 2

Is Copy Trading Allowed?

English

Copying other traders is usually not allowed in funding challenges. Funding systems want to evaluate your own skill, discipline, strategy, and risk management.

Copy trading can lead to account failure if it is prohibited by the provider.

ትግርኛ

ናይ ካልኦት ትሬደራት ትሬድ ምቕዳሕ ኣብ Funding Challenges ብዙሕ ግዜ ኣይፍቀድን። Funding Systems ናይ ገዛእ ርእስኻ ክእለት፣ Discipline፣ Strategy ከምኡውን Risk Management ክፍትኑ ይደልዩ።

Provider እንተ ከልኪልዎ፣ Copy Trading Account Failure ከስዕብ ይኽእል።
Restriction 3

Is News Trading Allowed?

English

Trading solely based on news events is often restricted because it is considered high risk. However, many systems do not require traders to close existing positions during news events.

ትግርኛ

ብ News Events ጥራይ ተመርኲስካ ትሬድ ምግባር ብዙሕ ግዜ ይኽልከል፣ ምኽንያቱ ብጣዕሚ High Risk ስለ ዝኾነ። ግን ብዙሓት Systems ኣብ News Events ዘለዉ Positions ክዕጸዉ ግዴታ ኣይገብሩን።

Restriction 4

Can I Trade Spot?

English

Many crypto funding challenges are focused on futures trading. Spot trading may not be available unless the provider clearly allows it.

Do not trade spot if the challenge is futures-based.

ትግርኛ

ብዙሓት Crypto Funding Challenges ኣብ Futures Trading ዝተመስረቱ እዮም። Spot Trading Provider ብግልጺ እንተ ዘይፈቒዱ ።

Challenge Futures-based እንተ ኾይኑ፣ Spot Trading ኣይትግበር።

Restriction 6

Stop-Loss Obligation Breaches

English

  • No stop loss placed.
  • Stop loss not placed within 5 minutes.
  • Risk exceeds 3% of the initial account balance.
  • Stop loss is cancelled without adjusting the position.
Most funding systems treat stop-loss violations seriously.

ትግርኛ

  • Stop Loss ዘይምቕማጥ።
  • Stop Loss ኣብ 5 ደቓይቕ ውሽጢ ዘይምቕማጥ።
  • Risk ካብ 3% ናይ Initial Balance ምብላጽ።
  • Position ከይተስተኻኸለ Stop Loss ምስራዝ።
ብዙሓት Funding Systems Stop-Loss Violations ብጣዕሚ ከቢድ ይርእይዎ።
Restriction 7

Correct Way to Set a Stop Loss

English

You may open a trade first, but many systems require the stop loss to be placed within 5 minutes. To be valid, the stop loss should usually be set using the platform’s TP/SL function.

  • Accepted: Stop Loss
  • Accepted: Trailing Stop Loss if supported
  • Not accepted: Conditional triggers
  • Not accepted: Limit close orders
A market stop-loss is often safer than a conditional limit order because it is more likely to execute during fast market movement.

ትግርኛ

ትሬድ ቅድሚ Stop Loss ክትከፍት ትኽእል፣ ግን ብዙሓት Systems Stop Loss ኣብ 5 ደቓይቕ ውሽጢ ክቕመጥ ይጠልቡ። ሓቀኛ ክኸውን፣ ብዙሕ ግዜ Stop Loss ብ TP/SL Function ክቕመጥ ኣለዎ።

  • ዝፍቀድ: Stop Loss
  • ዝፍቀድ: Trailing Stop Loss እንተ ተደጊፉ
  • ዘይፍቀድ: Conditional Triggers
  • ዘይፍቀድ: Limit Close Orders
Market Stop-Loss ብዙሕ ግዜ ካብ Conditional Limit Order ዝሓሸ እዩ፣ ምኽንያቱ ኣብ ሓያል Market Movement ክፍጸም ዝያዳ ዕድል ኣለዎ።
