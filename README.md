# Obsession Flow Terminal V2

### Multi-Asset Trading Dashboard | Financial Market Technology

A web-based trading and financial market analysis platform developed to combine market monitoring, technical analysis, macroeconomic indicators, and trading education in one interface.

**[🌐 Live Trading Terminal](https://lalimba.github.io/obsession-terminal/)**

## Project Overview

Obsession Flow Terminal is an independently developed financial technology project focused on providing traders with an integrated environment for studying market behavior and analyzing multiple asset classes.

The platform combines trading dashboards, market information, charting tools, and educational resources.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Asset Monitoring:** Bitcoin, Ethereum, Gold, and other financial markets.
- **Technical Analysis:** Interactive charting, technical indicators, and market structure analysis.
- **Macroeconomic Analysis:** DXY, US10Y, VIX, and economic market information.
- **Market Data:** Funding rates, open interest, and other available market indicators.
- **Trading Education:** Integrated learning resources for BTC, Gold, and cryptocurrency markets.
- **Research Tools:** Specialized dashboards for market bias, liquidity, and trading strategy exploration.
- **Responsive Interface:** Browser-based access to trading tools and dashboards.
