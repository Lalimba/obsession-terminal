ATR Risk System

ATR Risk System - ናይ ሓደጋን SL / TP መለክዒ

English Explanation

  • ATR measures volatility.
  • It helps us avoid too-tight stop losses.
  • High ATR means the market is moving strongly.
  • Low ATR means the market is slow or compressed.

ብትግርኛ መግለጺ

  • ATR ቅልጣፌ ዕዳጋ ይመዝን።
  • SL ኣዝዩ ጸቢብ ከይኸውን ይሕግዘና።
  • ATR ልዑል እንተኾይኑ ዕዳጋ ብሓይሊ ይንቀሳቐስ።
  • ATR ትሑት እንተኾይኑ ዕዳጋ ዝሑል ወይ compress እዩ።

Practical Example

ATR is like the speed of a car. EMA tells direction; ATR tells speed.

Gold V34 Rule

Do not use this factor alone. Combine it with trend, DXY, session, momentum, FVG, liquidity, ATR risk, and multi-timeframe confirmation.
