Multi Timeframes - 1H / 4H / Daily ምርግጋጽ

English Explanation

  • Multi-timeframe confirmation avoids confusion.
  • Daily gives the big direction.
  • 4H gives the main trading bias.
  • 1H helps refine entry zones.

ብትግርኛ መግለጺ

  • Multi-timeframe ምርግጋጽ ምድንጋር ይንክይ።
  • Daily ዓቢ ኣንፈት ይህብ።
  • 4H ቀንዲ trading bias ይህብ።
  • 1H ናይ entry ቦታ ንምጽራይ ይሕግዝ።

Practical Example

If 4H is bearish, do not take every small 1H bullish move as a real reversal.

Gold V34 Rule

Do not use this factor alone. Combine it with trend, DXY, session, momentum, FVG, liquidity, ATR risk, and multi-timeframe confirmation.
